## Audit Question

After `thm:generated-collection-closure-laws`, can we immediately define:

```text
topology generated by E
sigma-algebra generated by E
Borel sigma-algebra generated by the open sets
```

The answer is: not yet.  The abstract generated-collection theorem gives the
intersection mechanism, but topology and sigma-algebras require concrete
closure predicates.

## Missing Bridge

The missing bridge is not cardinality, descriptive set theory, or the Borel
hierarchy.  The missing bridge is operational closure:

- closed under pairwise unions;
- closed under pairwise intersections;
- closed under complements;
- closed under finite unions;
- closed under finite intersections;
- closed under countable unions;
- closed under countable intersections;
- closed under arbitrary unions where topology needs it;
- intersections of all systems satisfying a concrete closure package still
  satisfy that package.

Without these, `sigma(E)` and generated topology would feel like magic names
instead of instances of the generated-collection theorem.

## Decision

Do not add a standalone generated-topology theorem in Volume I yet.

Do not add a standalone generated-sigma-algebra theorem in Volume I yet.

Instead, proceed in this order:

1. Closure-rule vocabulary and closure-system examples.
2. Finite algebras of sets.
3. Generated finite algebras.
4. Sigma-algebras and countable closure.
5. Generated sigma-algebra and its universal property.
6. Borel sigma-algebra as `sigma(open sets)`.
7. Euclidean Borel toolkit for `R` and `R^n`.

## Why This Order Matters

Generated structures are intersections of all admissible structures containing
some seed family.  The word "admissible" is doing real work.  For topologies,
admissible means arbitrary-union and finite-intersection closure.  For
sigma-algebras, admissible means complement and countable-union closure.

The current theorem tells us how generation behaves once admissibility is
encoded as a closure system.  The next job is to learn the admissibility
conditions themselves.
