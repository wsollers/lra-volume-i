# Gate 57: Borel Sigma-Algebra on R^n

## Target

Introduce `def:borel-sigma-algebra-rn`.

## Statement Shape

Let \(d_{\mathrm{Euc}}\) be the Euclidean metric on \(\mathbb R^n\), and let
\(\tau_{\mathrm{Euc}}\) be the metric topology induced by that metric.  The
Borel sigma-algebra on \(\mathbb R^n\) is

\[
  \mathcal B(\mathbb R^n)=\sigma(\tau_{\mathrm{Euc}}).
\]

Equivalently, it is the smallest sigma-algebra on \(\mathbb R^n\) containing
every Euclidean open set.

## Why This Gate Exists

This is the exact set-family object used later in measure theory and
multivariable analysis.

The chain is:

1. Euclidean distance on \(\mathbb R^n\);
2. Euclidean open balls;
3. Euclidean open sets;
4. Borel sigma-algebra generated by those open sets.

## Proof Status

The Lean definition is intentionally parameterized by a Euclidean distance

## Common Mistakes

- Thinking \(\mathcal B(\mathbb R^n)\) is the topology itself.  It is the
  sigma-algebra generated by the topology.
- Trying to enumerate all Borel sets.  The tactical definition is by
  generation.
- Chasing cardinality or Borel hierarchy facts here.  Those are deliberately
  outside this chapter.
