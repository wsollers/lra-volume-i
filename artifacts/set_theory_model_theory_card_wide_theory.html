Native and Unlocked Interface

Native variables, equality, logic, quantifiers, membership ∈, and the empty-set constant ∅
Axiom-Given ∅, unordered pairs {a,b}, unions over set-families, power sets 𝒫(A), infinite sets, separated subsets, replacement images, selector sets
Derived A∪B, A∩B, A\B, Aᶜ relative to U, A△B, A×B
Later Structure relations, functions, images, preimages, order, lattices, cardinality, quotient objects
Reading: the language supplies ∈ and ∅; a structure interprets them as E and e; the theory ZFC is the axiom inventory that makes the interpreted structure behave like a universe of sets.
